# Preview-first outreach email

The preview is already built, so the first email includes the link. Write in the recipient's language and register (formal by default, informal only if the business clearly uses it). Status is always draft until a human approves the exact batch.

## Template (adapt wording to the language)

Subject: Quick question about <business>'s website

Hello <Title Name>,

<Opening line built from real, recurring themes in the business's public reviews>, but your website does not reflect that yet.

I took the liberty of designing how your website could look. It also makes you easier to find on search and in AI assistants.

You can see it here:
<link>

You are welcome to keep the design. If you would like help putting it live, I am happy to help.

Best regards,
<Sender>

## Opening line rule
- Paraphrase what customers actually say, only if a theme shows up in at least three separate reviews. Keep the raw reviews as evidence.
- No star ratings, review counts, or numbers. No invented praise.
- If no theme repeats, fall back to the safest honest line.

## Greeting
Use the owner name from the business's own legal or about page. If no person is published, use a neutral greeting.

## Rules
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use commas or periods.
- Check that every link returns 200 before sending.
- Because the email contains a link, send clean HTML anchors through your approved send path (see `email-send-self-test`), not a draft card that rewrites links.
- Send one at a time and stop at the first failure.
- Never send without the owner's explicit yes for that exact batch.
