# Free build plus retainer (generic offer)

Fill the placeholders from your own pricing decisions. The owner decides price and scope, never a bot.

## Offer
- **Build:** a free one-page site for a local business with a missing or dead site.
- **Retainer:** <monthly price> for hosting, uptime, and light edits.
- **Fulfillment:** inbound message, triaged into a ticket, fulfilled by the doer, reviewed by the desk.
- **Out of scope at the retainer price:** redesigns, custom apps, SEO projects, anything that needs the owner by default.
- **Upsell:** a larger package when they need a real store or app.

## Positioning
- A front door into your studio, not a second company.
- Same ideal customer as your other outreach: owner-run, not chains.
- Do not undercut your main package on price. This is the brochure-site product.

## Ops rules
- Cap edits to light copy and image changes per month.
- Hard spend caps at every provider.
- Gate the first few outbound runs for human review, then relax only if quality holds.
- Keep books and spend separate from your main package.

## Do not
- Quote a retainer below what the work costs to fulfil.
- Mix custom build work into the retainer.
- Auto-post from the owner's personal accounts for this offer.
- Send anything to real prospects without the owner's approval of that batch.
